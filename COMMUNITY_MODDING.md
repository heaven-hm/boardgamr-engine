# Community modding and responsible use

Heaven-HM encourages creative, educational, accessible, preservation-focused,
and community-friendly mods. We ask users to create and share mods freely
and avoid selling mods or charging for access to this tool.

Do not use this project to commit or knowingly facilitate unlawful activity,
distribute unlawfully obtained material, infringe others' rights, deploy
malware, gain unauthorized access, or intentionally cause real-world harm to
people, property, or data. Fictional events within games are not real-world
harm. Comply with applicable law and obtain required permissions for game
code, assets, and trademarks.
